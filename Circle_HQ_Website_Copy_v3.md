### The Problems We Solve

**1. Manual Drag Slowing Efficiency**
Teams lose hours a day to repetitive, manual work — retyping information between systems, chasing approvals, updating spreadsheets by hand.
**Our Fix:** Workflow and approval automation that removes repetitive manual steps, so work moves without waiting on people.

**2. Loss of Real-Time Information & Poor Documentation**
Decisions get made on outdated numbers because information is scattered across spreadsheets, WhatsApp threads, and someone's memory — not in one place, and not up to date.
**Our Fix:** Real-time reporting and business intelligence dashboards that centralise your data and surface it as it happens.

**3. Missed Inquiries & Slow Response Times**
Customer messages that arrive after hours, over the weekend, or during a busy period go unanswered for too long — and every unanswered message is a lost opportunity.
**Our Fix:** 24/7 AI chat agents that respond instantly across every channel, and escalate to a human only when it's genuinely needed.

**4. Inconsistent Onboarding & Drop-Off**
New clients, tenants, or staff are often left to figure things out themselves, with no repeatable process — leading to confusion, delays, and drop-off.
**Our Fix:** Automated onboarding flows that deliver the right information and the right next step, the same way, every time.

**5. No Visibility Into Revenue or Performance**
Leadership finds out how the business is really performing weeks after the fact, once someone has had time to compile a report.
**Our Fix:** Automated revenue and performance tracking systems that show what's coming in, where, and when — as it happens.

---

- **Any other area where your business needs AI to accelerate results.** If your specific need is not listed above, we encourage you to reach out.

*Business AI Literacy Programme — Corporate & Organisational Training*

Designed for organisations that want their teams working more effectively alongside AI — understanding how to use it responsibly, safely, and strategically. This is not a technical course. ### Individual Skill-Based Training
*AI Agent Builder Programme*

For individuals and teams who want to go beyond understanding AI and start building with it. Takes participants from zero to functional AI agent builders — capable of designing and deploying real automation workflows using tools like Make, Zapier, and n8n.

**Format:** Online, live + self-paced modules
**Cohort size:** Minimum 20 per cohort, rolling enrolment
**Practicals:** 3 real agent builds per participant
**Availability:** Custom arrangements available for corporate cohorts

**Three intensity tiers, same curriculum — the difference is pace, depth, and level of mentorship:**

- **Fast-Track** — One month, high-pace, live-led, best for self-starters with some prior AI exposure.
- **Standard** — Two months, balanced pace with deeper coverage and regular feedback, recommended for most participants.
- **Deep-Dive** — Three months, extended practicals, portfolio-grade builds, and individual mentorship — for those who want to come out job- or client-ready.

**By the end, participants can:**
- Build a WhatsApp or email auto-response workflow — no code required.
- Build a functional lead capture and CRM automation connected to a real business workflow.
- Design and deploy a complete AI agent handling a real business process end-to-end, with a portfolio of builds ready for client or employer use.

*Every cohort ends with a graduation ceremony that doubles as a job fair — giving organisations a direct route to hire from a pool of AI-ready graduates.*

# 6. FAQ

**What does Circle HQ actually build?**
Custom AI business systems — built around your specific operations, not sold as a fixed product. Examples include 24/7 chat agents, reporting and business intelligence dashboards, workflow and approval automation, customer onboarding automation, and revenue tracking systems. If your need isn't listed, we still want to hear about it.

**What's the difference between Circle AI Solutions and Circle Academy?**
Circle AI Solutions builds the AI systems your business runs on. Circle Academy trains the people who run them — either as an organisation-wide literacy programme, or as individual, hands-on AI agent-building training. You can work with one or both.

**Do I need existing systems in place before working with Circle HQ?**
No. Part of what we do in the consultation is assess where your business currently stands — including businesses with no formal systems at all — and recommend the right starting point.

**How long does it take to build a system?**
It depends entirely on what's being built. A simple automation can move quickly; a multi-system integration takes longer. You'll get a clear timeline as part of your roadmap, before any work begins.

**Is Circle Academy only for people with a technical background?**
No. The Business AI Literacy Programme is built for non-technical staff at any level. The AI Agent Builder Programme is more hands-on, but starts from the fundamentals — no prior coding experience required.

**Can my organisation book both a system build and staff training?**
Yes. Many organisations do both — building the system through Circle AI Solutions, and training their team through Circle Academy, so the people using the system are set up to get the most out of it.

**Do you work with businesses outside Lagos or outside Nigeria?**
Circle HQ works remotely with clients anywhere. Distance isn't a barrier to how we work.

**How do I get started?**
Book a consultation. It's a short, no-obligation conversation to understand your business and figure out the right next step — whether that's a system, training, or both.
